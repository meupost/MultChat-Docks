Use your business number for the connection and a second number to act as a customer. The MultChat labels below are shown in Portuguese.

## Set up the team

1. Open **Departamentos** and select **Novo departamento**. Create the queues your team uses, such as Sales or Support.
2. Open **Usuários**. Add team members or review their existing accounts.
3. Check each user's profile and queues. Use an account with permission to configure users, departments, and connections.

## Connect WhatsApp

1. Open **Conexões** and select **Nova conexão**.
2. Choose a connection type enabled for your company.
   - **WhatsApp by QR code**. Open **Linked devices** in WhatsApp on your business phone and scan the code displayed in MultChat.
   - **Official WhatsApp API**. Select the official channel and follow the steps in MultChat to link your number.
3. Open the connection details. Check that the channel is connected and that the selected queues match the team that will handle the conversations.

## Receive and reply to a test message

1. Send a message from another number to your business WhatsApp number.
2. Open **Atendimentos** and find the conversation by contact.
3. If it is waiting for a reply, select **Aceitar**.
4. Write and send a reply, then check that it arrived on the test number.

Use **Transferir atendimento** to send the conversation to an available queue or agent. To finish the test, select **Resolver atendimento** and follow the steps on screen.

## If the conversation does not appear

Check that the message went to the right number and the connection is active. In **Atendimentos**, review the filters. If you lack access to the expected queue, ask your administrator to check which queues your account can access.

To set up queues and route conversations from the WhatsApp menu, follow the [Departments tutorial](/en/organizar-equipe).

To learn the inbox and channel-specific actions, follow the [Conversations tutorial](/en/atender-conversas).
